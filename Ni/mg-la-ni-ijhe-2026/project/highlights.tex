\begin{itemize}
  \item Fixed 8 wt.\% La isolates the effects of 0--8 wt.\% Ni additions.
  \item Ni accelerates dehydrogenation with limited thermodynamic change.
  \item Activation energy decreases from 158.8 to 90.2 kJ mol$^{-1}$.
  \item At 340~$^{\circ}$C, $t_{90}$ decreases from 18 to about 2 min.
\end{itemize}
